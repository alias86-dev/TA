**SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS**
Total Annihilation: The Core Contingency (TACC) requires the full version of Total 
Annihilation as well as the following hardware specifications:

·	Windows 95
·	Pentium 100mHz/133 MHz recommended
·	32MB RAM
·	4x CD-ROM
·	Sound Blaster compatible audio system
·	VGA 256 color or better
·	Mouse



**INSTALLATION**
In order to play any component of TACC you must install that component onto your 
computer.  TACC will not read information directly from the CD-ROM.  For example, 
if you want to use a multiplayer map included on the TACC disc, you must first 
install the TACC multi-player map data to your hard drive.

To install TACC, insert the disc into your CD-ROM drive.  Make sure the full 
version of Total Annihilation is already installed on your machine.  The TACC 
installation will not continue until the full version of the game is installed.  
If Autoplay is enabled an installation screen will appear a few moments after 
inserting the disc.  Follow the directions as prompted by the install program.  
The installer will automatically find the appropriate Total Annihilation 
directory so there is no need to specify a path.  

If Autoplay is disabled, use Windows Explorer or the My Computer icon to locate 
the installation executable, called Setup.exe, on your CD-ROM drive.  Double-clicking 
Setup.exe will launch the install program.

Once installed you can start the game as usual, click SINGLE and then click 
CAMPAIGN.  'The Core Contingency' will be added to your list of available 
campaigns.  New maps will be listed alphabetically in the SELECT MAP dialog 
box in both Skirmish and Multiplayer.  Additionally, an icon will be added to 
the Start menu in the Total Annihilation directory allowing you to uninstall 
TACC.

**NEW MAPS & WORLDS**
The maps included with TACC feature a combination of original Total Annihilation 
environments as well as six new worlds, described in further detail below.  After 
installing TACC, the maps will be integrated alphabetically into the SELECT MAP 
dialog box available in Skirmish and Multiplayer.  All maps are designed for both 
single and multiplayer use and vary in size and number of players.

Acid World
The most striking feature of Acid World is its unusual plant life.  The reclaimable, 
energy-rich gasbags are tethered to the ground and float freely in response to 
the world's high winds.  Green oceans and lakes can support sea units but the 
corrosive acid does gradual damage to these units.  The acid does not affect 
hover units which skim lightly above the surface.

Crystal World
An inhospitable world with sharp, jagged terrain, Crystal World offers few reclaimable 
terrain features.  Ground units may find the ground rough and challenging to navigate.  
Although unusual in color, the purple oceans are non-corrosive and can support sea 
bases.

Lusch World
Like the name suggests, Lusch World features a rich, diverse topography.  Energy is 
available from dense forests and plant life.  The terrain varies from swamps to high, 
ragged cliffs and steep mountains.

Slate World
Access to sections of Slate World are limited by impassable clouds which only air 
units can traverse.  There are no plant but metal is readily available from rocks and 
deposits.  Look for the richest metal deposits in crevasses and rock fissures.

Urban World
Remnants of a decimated Arm civilization are visible throughout Urban World.  
Reclamation of these fertile resources becomes the primary means to maintaining a 
metal income here.  Deposits are only to be found in the city parks, which are few.  
Metal deposits are also available underwater in the rivers and lakes.

Water World
A landless world with high, strong tides, Water World is designed for sea battles.  
Metal can be obtained from the rich underwater deposits.  Scattered throughout the 
water are coral reefs.  The reefs are not reclaimable but do affect unit movement.  
Seaplanes can be an effective complement to your water base on this world.



**NEW FEATURES**
TACC includes several new features which have been added to the game.  These are 
described in further detail here.

SHORTCUT KEYS
	CTRL + M	Select all mines
	CTRL + P	Select all aircraft with weapons
	CTRL + R	Select all radar, radar jammers, sonar and sonar jammers
	CTRL + W	Select all mobile units with weapons except Commander

MULTIPLAYER SHARING
Control the resources you share with your teammates and allies using the following 
shortcut commands, listed below.  These commands can be used in multiplayer by hitting 
ENTER to bring up the message bar on the Main Battle Screen and typing in any of the 
following phrases in bold type below:

	+shareenergy		Shares energy
	+sharemetal		Shares metal
	+sharemapping		Shares map information

You can also control the amount of metal and energy shared to your teammates and 
allies by modifying these commands slightly with a numerical value.  X indicates 
the amount of resource left in your reserve.  For example, +sharemetal 5000 will 
assure that you have 5000 metal in your economy at all times. You cannot set the 
amount of resources you share any higher than your current storage capacity.  
Make sure you leave a space between the command and the numerical value.  

	+setshareenergy X	Shares energy while keeping X amount for yourself
	+setsharemetal X	Shares metal while keeping X amount for yourself

SHIFT & CONSTRUCTION UNITS
While placing your cursor over any construction unit and then holding down the SHIFT 
key, square outlines may appear on the map.  Green squares represent the build orders 
for the unit currently selected.  Blue squares represent the build orders for all 
other construction units.  These squares can be used as a guideline when queuing up 
build orders for a construction unit.

SHIFT & CLOAKABLE UNITS
Placing your cursor over a cloaked unit and then holding down the SHIFT key will display 
a white circle.  This circle represents that unit's minimum cloaking radius.  When an 
enemy unit moves into that radius, the cloaked unit becomes visible.

SHIFT & MINES
To view the properties of a mine, hold down SHIFT while your cursor is over the unit.  
The stationary red circle denotes the mine's trigger area.  The animated red circle 
represents its blast radius.  These both vary in range for each mine.

FACTORIES & SQUADS
Select a factory and assign it a squad number.  This can be done by pressing CTRL+1 
through 9.  All units produced by that factory will automatically be assigned to the 
same squad.

KROGOTH MISSION
The Krogoth mission is a special bonus mission designed for advanced players.  It 
is possible to change the difficulty setting to Easy or Medium but this does not 
affect the difficulty of the mission.

TAE INSTALLATION
To install TAE, use Windows Explorer to locate the installation executable called 
TAEsetup.exe on your TACC CD-ROM, located in the TAE subdirectory.  Double-click 
TAEsetup.exe to install the editor.  Follow the dialog boxes as prompted by the 
install program.  TAE must be installed in the same directory as Total Annihilation.
